# Project Scope

## Overview

CESE-RLIM (Research, Laboratory & Innovation Management Platform) is specifically designed for the Center of Excellence for Electrical Systems and Electronics (CESE) at Adama Science and Technology University (ASTU).

This platform focuses on **operational research resources and activities**, NOT on research project lifecycle management.

## In Scope

### Researcher Management
- Researcher profiles and information
- Academic positions and departments
- Research areas and expertise
- ORCID integration
- Employee/student ID tracking

### Laboratory Management
- Laboratory information and locations
- Laboratory status (Active, Inactive, Under Maintenance)
- Capacity management
- Responsible person assignment
- Equipment inventory per lab

### Equipment Management
- Equipment inventory and cataloging
- Asset tracking with unique IDs
- Serial number management
- Equipment categories and specifications
- Purchase information and warranty tracking
- Condition monitoring (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Damaged)
- Status tracking (Available, Reserved, In Use, Under Maintenance, Damaged, Lost, Retired)

### Equipment Requests
- Request submission with purpose and dates
- Priority levels (Low, Medium, High, Urgent)
- Review and approval workflow
- Request status tracking
- Research project association

### Equipment Assignments
- Equipment issuing and returning
- Issue/return condition tracking
- Expected return date management
- Researcher and project assignment

### Maintenance
- Maintenance request reporting
- Technician assignment
- Problem diagnosis and action tracking
- Maintenance status workflow
- Cost tracking

### Research Project References
- Basic project information (code, title, status)
- Resource-project relationships
- **NOT** full project lifecycle management

### Innovation Management
- Innovation submission and tracking
- Development stage tracking (Idea → Prototype → Testing → Validated → Transferred)
- Evaluation status workflow
- Research project association

### Notifications
- System notifications
- Read/unread status
- Notification types (Info, Warning, Success, Error, Request, Maintenance)

### Audit Trail
- User action logging
- Entity change tracking
- IP address and user agent logging
- Metadata capture

### Dashboard & Reports
- Overview statistics
- Resource utilization metrics
- Activity feeds
- (Reports module to be expanded in future)

### Authentication & Authorization
- JWT-based authentication
- Role-based access control (Admin, Coordinator, Researcher, Technician)
- Protected API routes
- Session management

## Out of Scope

### Research Proposal Management
- Proposal submission and templates
- Proposal review workflows
- Proposal approval chains
- Proposal versioning

### Research Funding Management
- Grant applications
- Budget tracking
- Financial reporting
- Fund disbursement

### Publication Management
- Publication tracking
- Citation management
- Journal/conference information
- Impact factor tracking

### University-Wide Systems
- Student academic management
- HR/Payroll systems
- Procurement workflows
- Financial management
- Curriculum management

### Other
- Student academic records
- Course management
- Examination systems
- Library management
- Attendance tracking

## Platform Focus

CESE-RLIM is an **internal enterprise platform** for managing the operational resources and research innovation activities of CESE. It is NOT a:

- Student project management tool
- Academic management system
- Research proposal tracking system
- Financial management system

The platform is designed to be:

- Professional and institutional
- Focused on resource management
- Easy to use for researchers and staff
- Scalable for future module additions
- Secure and auditable
